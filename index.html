About

A technology leader who builds teams, delivers BMS & RMS platforms at scale, and turns complex broadcast challenges into measurable outcomes.

On Leadership: I lead cross-functional teams of engineers, implementation specialists, and client success managers — setting technical direction, defining KPIs, and building a culture where people take ownership. I report directly to the Board, own the technology budget and roadmap, and conduct C-level business reviews with clients across four continents — a mandate that today spans Warner Bros. Discovery USA's 50+ application AdTech ecosystem and the RightsU rollout at JioStar. Over 15 years and five roles at the same company, I grew from an individual contributor to Head of Media Technology — a trajectory built on trust, results, and the ability to develop the people around me.

On BMS Implementation: I have led 12 end-to-end Broadcast Management System deployments for organisations including Sony Pictures Networks, Viacom18, SABC, Warner Bros. Discovery, and Tata Play. My BMS expertise covers broadcast scheduling, ad sales traffic management, rights clearance, EPG generation, and playout integration. From greenfield rollouts to complex migrations (including BroadView transitions and AWS cloud migrations), I ensure every implementation delivers operational reliability and commercial value.

On RMS & ERP Integration: I architect and deliver Rights Management System implementations that give broadcasters full control over content licensing, territorial rights, and compliance — currently leading the RightsU implementation at JioStar, India's largest broadcaster. I also bridge RMS and BMS with enterprise ERP systems — integrating SAP for finance, invoicing, amortisation, and revenue assurance. This end-to-end approach eliminates silos and gives leadership a single source of truth across rights, scheduling, and revenue.

On Impact: My teams have reduced operational costs by 40% through automation and process re-engineering, achieved 99.9% broadcast uptime through HA/DR architecture, and increased response efficiency by 30% through AI-powered workflows and self-service portals. I've delivered solutions across India, the UAE, South Africa, Papua New Guinea, and the USA — always with a disciplined, client-first approach.

How the work gets done

SABC · Johannesburg · Go-Live Dec 2024

Migrating a national broadcaster to BroadView — without going off air

Challenge

The South African Broadcasting Corporation needed its channels moved onto the BroadView BMS — scheduling, traffic, and ad sales — while daily broadcast operations continued uninterrupted.

Approach

As senior SME and project lead in Johannesburg, I ran the end-to-end channel migration: planning, configuration, data migration, UAT, and cutover, working hands-on with SABC's operational teams.

Result

Go-live in December 2024, with the national broadcaster's channels scheduled, trafficked, and monetized on BroadView.

Sony Pictures Networks · BroadView on AWS · Feb 2023

Lifting a mission-critical BMS estate into AWS

Challenge

Hosted on-premise infrastructure was constraining uptime, disaster recovery, and access for globally distributed teams.

Approach

Led the migration of the BroadView platform to AWS — re-architecting hosting and HA/DR, opening secure global access, and shifting infrastructure spend from CAPEX to OPEX.

Result

Improved uptime and disaster recovery for mission-critical broadcast systems, and a cloud foundation central to the ~40% operational cost reduction achieved across automation and cloud initiatives.

Client Services · 2023–24

Rebuilding client support around AI

Challenge

Routine support queries were consuming specialist engineering time and slowing response for broadcast clients operating around the clock.

Approach

Rolled out AI-assisted support workflows and a support chatbot, and launched a self-service client portal with AI-driven search for common issues.

Result

Response speed improved by about 30%, manual escalations fell, and clients now resolve routine issues without raising a ticket.
